About Our School

Delhi Public School is a private school that focuses on good education, discipline and overall development of every student. We help children grow in studies, sports and creativity.

Our teachers give personal attention to every child, and our campus gives students a safe place to learn, play and make friends. From the first day in school to the board exams, we guide each student with care.

  • Experienced and caring teachers
  • Safe and clean campus
  • Focus on studies, sports and activities

Our Mission

To give every child quality education and the confidence to face the world.

Our Vision

To be a school where students grow into responsible, kind and successful citizens.

Our Values

Honesty, discipline, respect, teamwork and a love for learning.
